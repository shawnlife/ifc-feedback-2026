Privacy

This feedback form is anonymous unless you choose otherwise. You don't need a name, email address or login to use it, and we don't use cookies or tracking.

Who is responsible

The Resource Alliance (registered charity in England and Wales, no. 1099889) runs the International Fundraising Congress and is responsible for this data. Questions or requests: contact@resource-alliance.org.

What we collect

  • The session you chose (or the name you typed in) and your answers
  • The time you sent it
  • Whether you opened the form from the QR code, a link or your home screen
  • Simple counts of clicks on the help and credit links, with nothing about who clicked

We do not collect your IP address, location or device details, and we don't collect your name or email unless you give them as described below.

If you leave your contact details: at the end of the form you can tick a box and leave your name and email in case the IFC team needs to follow up on your feedback. This is optional and only applies to that one response. Your details are kept with that response, seen only by the IFC team (never by speakers), and used only to follow up on your feedback. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by emailing us, and we'll remove them.

Session Leaders (the separate Session Leader form) are asked for their name, so the IFC team knows whose report it is. It is only seen by the IFC team, and your phone remembers it so you don't have to retype it for each session.

Masterclass picks (the separate page for Session Leaders to choose their top three masterclasses) asks you to choose your name from a list so the IFC team can allocate masterclasses. Only the IFC team sees your picks.

Why

To understand how sessions landed, give speakers feedback, and make future IFC events better. Our lawful basis is the Resource Alliance's legitimate interest in improving the congress.

Where it is kept and for how long

Answers are stored in Google Firebase and a Google Sheet (Google acts as our processor) that only the IFC team can open. We keep responses to compare with future events. The web page itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, briefly keeps standard technical logs for security.

Your comments

Please don't include personal details in written answers. If something you wrote could identify you and you'd like it removed, email us with the session and roughly when you sent it, and we'll delete it.

Stored on your phone

The form saves two small notes on your own device, never shared with anyone: which sessions you've already rated (to show a tick), and any answer that hasn't been sent yet because of a weak connection, so it isn't lost. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what we hold, ask us to correct or delete it, and complain to a data protection authority (in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). As most responses are anonymous, we can usually only find a specific one if you tell us the session and time, or the email address you left.
