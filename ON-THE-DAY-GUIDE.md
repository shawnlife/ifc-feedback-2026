# Session feedback: guide for the day

You only ever need **the Google Sheet**. You never need to touch the website.

## The Sheet has four tabs

| Tab | What it's for | Can I edit it? |
|---|---|---|
| **Sessions** | The programme the form searches | **Yes**, this is your job |
| **Responses** | Every piece of feedback, one row each | Read only. Don't change row 1 |
| **Test responses** | Tests sent before the event | Ignore |
| **Summary** | Averages per session | Rebuilt from the menu |

## A session changed (new room, new time, speaker swap, typo)

1. Open the **Sessions** tab.
2. Change the cell. That's it.
3. The form shows the change within about a minute. Attendees who already have the page open get it next time they come back to it.

Keep times as `14:00` and dates as `2026-10-14` (or `14/10/2026`).

## A session was added

Add a new row at the bottom. Fill in at least **Title**, **Room**, **Date** and **Start**. Leave **ID** empty, it's made automatically.

## A session was cancelled

Delete its row. Any feedback already given stays in Responses.

## After any change

Menu **IFC Feedback > Check the session list for problems**. It either says "All good" or lists the rows to fix.

## Keep the dashboard open

Have the dashboard open on one laptop during session times. Every refresh (automatic, once a minute) pulls the newest responses into the Sheet, so results stay live. Without it the Sheet still updates on its own every 5 minutes between 07:00 and 22:00.

## Seeing results

Menu **IFC Feedback > Update the Summary tab**. You get one row per session with the number of responses and the average of each star question. Run it again whenever you want fresh numbers.

For the detail (every comment), use the **Responses** tab. Tip: Data > Create a filter, then filter the Session column.

## What attendees see if they can't find their session

There's a "My session isn't listed" link. They type the name themselves, and the response lands in Responses with the Note column saying **Typed in by attendee**. Worth a glance once a day in case a session is missing from the list.

## If someone says "it didn't send"

Wifi at the venue drops sometimes. The form keeps the answer on their phone and sends it automatically next time they open the page with signal. Nothing is lost. Ask them to open the page again once they have connection.

## Please don't

- Rename the tabs or the headings in row 1.
- Share the Sheet publicly (the comments are internal).
- Change anything under Extensions > Apps Script.
